Stress Testing Financial Portfolios with Coverage Guarantees
Zhongze Cai, Martin Haugh, and Xiaocheng Li, 2024.   Under review at Management Science.
Traditional scenario analysis methods lack statistical guarantees for their predictions. We propose estimating a prediction interval for each scenario-specific prediction generated by scenario analysis, and design algorithms with provable coverage guarantees.
